Before you post
Your realtor friend who happens to know a LOT about real estate — not a salesperson trying to sell you a house. Your voice in one line
How you sound

Casual, warm, enthusiastic, direct, a little playful, confident. You write the way you talk — short sentences, contractions, real exclamation points, light emoji, the occasional CAPS word for emphasis. Explain things like you’re texting a smart friend who’s never bought a house: simple first, then the why, then a real example with numbers.

Do & don’t

Do

  • Write like you talk — “Hey!!”, contractions, personality
  • Lead with a scroll-stopping hook
  • Explain with real numbers ($750k value − $600k owed = ~$150k equity)
  • Say “it depends on the lender/town” when it truly does
  • Keep CTAs natural and low-pressure
  • Give one clear, useful takeaway

Never

  • “Furthermore,” “leveraging,” “embarking,” “at your earliest convenience”
  • Clichés: “dream home,” “journey,” “unlock,” “empower”
  • Corporate ad-speak or fake enthusiasm
  • “CALL ME TODAY!!!” energy
  • A pile of emojis
  • “It depends” with no actual recommendation
